20-Minute Latency Gap

Signal travel delays to Lunar orbit or deep space range from 2.5 seconds to 20+ minutes. If an astronaut skips a sterile sealing step or swaps toxic chemicals, Earth cannot alert them until the sample is already ruined.

Zero-G Posture Breakdown

Standard pose models assume an Earth floor and gravitational "down." In microgravity, astronauts float upside-down, sideways, or pitched at awkward angles. Ground-based HAR completely fails to recognize actions.

The PARIKSHAK Architecture

How PARIKSHAK Delivers Aerospace-Grade Verification

A mathematically grounded, offline-first pipeline built to aerospace reliability standards: Watch → Reason → Verify → Intervene → Seal.

🎯

1. Rack-Relative 3D Frame

Coordinates are resolved relative to AprilTag / YOLO fiducials bonded to the payload rack face. "Up" is defined by the experiment rack, not gravity. Even if the astronaut floats upside down, geometry remains deterministic.

Gravity-Independent Kinematics
👁️

2. Multimodal Neural Perception

High-speed YOLOv8 object detection paired with 17-point astronaut skeletal tracking and contact proximity analysis. Recognizes small spaceflight items (vials, boxes, latches, syringes) with spatial hand-object contact.

YOLOv8 + 17-Point Skeletal Model
📄

3. Zero-Retraining PDL Engine

Procedures are defined in declarative YAML (Procedure Definition Language). Adding a brand-new space experiment requires zero neural retraining—only a procedure file validated against the sensor capability contract.

Zero Neural Retraining
🔊

4. Causal Spoken Voice Alerts

Sub-second text-to-speech engine speaks clear verbal interventions. Crucially, it provides the causal reason: "Step eight, close the glovebox latch, was not completed. Close latch before starting unit."

Sub-2s Spoken Interventions
🔐

5. Tamper-Evident Flight Log

Every transition, step outcome, and biometric state is committed to a SHA-256 hash-chained JSONL log. Altering even one byte flags the exact tampered record, giving ground flight controllers cryptographically verified proof.

Cryptographic Tamper-Evident Chain
📡

6. Dual Video Streaming & Storage

Supports live GStreamer / RTSP IP streaming to mission control alongside local high-resolution storage in recordings/ with automated ±10s anomaly clip extraction around detected deviations.

IP Stream + Anomaly Clips
