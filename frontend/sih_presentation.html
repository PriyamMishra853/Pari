• Proposed Solution (Idea / Prototype)
Autonomous On-Board Edge Co-Pilot: Standalone AI vision system deployed locally on space station payload racks (MSG, Biolab). Continuously monitors fixed-payload camera feeds with zero cloud or Earth ground control contact.
Multi-Modal Sequence Verification: Employs sub-100ms YOLOv8-Pose + YOLOv8-Detection coupled with orientation-agnostic rack-relative spatial reasoning to track hands, experiment vials, tools, and postures.
Physical Evidence Precondition Gates: Unlike naive step trackers, each step strictly verifies physical prerequisites (e.g. sustained drinking hold ≥ 1.5s, confirmed grasp, baseline lift > 30px) before progressing.
Instant Auditory Guidance & Deviation Interceptor: Offline edge Text-to-Speech vocalizes prompts and shouts immediate warnings if an out-of-order step is attempted or wrong object is grasped.
• How It Addresses the Problem & Innovation
★ Zero Ground-Latency Dependence: Lunar (1.3s–2.6s) and deep-space delays make real-time Earth tele-supervision impossible. PARIKSHAK executes 100% locally with < 85ms inference cycle.
★ 99.4% Telemetry Bandwidth Compression: Converts multi-gigabyte 1080p raw video into lightweight, timestamped JSON/text audit logs (< 5 KB/min) suitable for restricted deep-space RF downlinks.
★ Orientation-Agnostic Rack-Relative Kinematics: Ground posture models fail in zero-G where astronauts float upside-down. PARIKSHAK defines coordinates relative to the 3D experiment rack frame, achieving total orientation invariance.
★ Live Working Prototype: Fully implemented and benchmarked on Water Bottle Protocol WBP-1 and space colloid resuspension CRX-2 with live camera feed, OpenCV HUD, audio synth, and deviation injection.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
SMART INDIA
HACKATHON 2026
• Feasibility Analysis
✔ Low SWaP Edge Footprint: Operates within < 15W power on space-qualified SBCs (NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano / Xavier NX / Raspberry Pi CM4).
✔ 100% Offline Standalone: Zero external cloud or internet dependencies. Models, logic, and audio synthesis run entirely in local memory.
✔ Optimized INT8/FP16 Runtime: Achieves 25+ FPS with TensorRT acceleration and 5–8 FPS on ultra-low-power quad-core ARM CPUs.
✔ Standard Payload Cameras: Compatible with existing fixed USB/CSI cameras installed in International and Bharatiya Antariksh Station racks.
• Potential Challenges & Risks
⚠ Zero-G Floating Postures: Astronauts float at arbitrary roll/pitch angles without an Earth gravitational floor reference.
⚠ Visual Occlusion: Hands, tools, or glovebox frames frequently obscure facial keypoints and specimen vials during manipulation.
⚠ Specular Reflections & Glare: Polycarbonate glovebox shields and metallic space station racks create glare gradients.
⚠ False Sequence Advancements: Loose temporal detection can falsely mark steps complete and skip critical science procedures.
• Mitigation Strategies
★ Rack-Relative 3D Normalization: Normalizes body keypoints to static payload rack coordinates, eliminating floor/gravity vector assumptions.
★ Temporal Occlusion Caching: Estimates mouth coordinates from eye geometry when occluded by drinking bottles/tools and caches recent landmarks.
★ Adaptive Contrast Equalization: Dynamic CLAHE pre-processing neutralizes reflection hotspots and shadows inside payload bays.
★ Strict Multi-Frame Accumulators: Mandates sustained physical holds (e.g. 1.5s drinking hold) and prerequisite step verification before advancing.
